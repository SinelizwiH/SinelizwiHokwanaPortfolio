# SinelizwiHokwanaPortfolio

Create a modern, professional, visually impressive personal portfolio website for Sinelizwi Hokwana, a South African Business Information Technology graduate and aspiring Software Developer, Business Analyst, and AI-enabled technology professional.

The portfolio should feel personal, confident, intelligent, creative, and future-focused. It should present Sinelizwi as someone who combines technical development skills with business understanding, problem-solving, creativity, entrepreneurship, and an interest in emerging AI technologies.

Overall Personal Brand

Position Sinelizwi as:

Business IT Graduate | Software Developer | Business Analyst | AI Enthusiast

The website should communicate that she is a technology professional who enjoys building practical digital solutions to real-world problems.

Use a clean, modern, premium technology aesthetic. The design should feel professional enough for recruiters and employers, but also creative and personal. Avoid making it look like a generic template.

Use a responsive design that works beautifully on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Hero Section

Create a strong hero section with:

* A large circular profile picture placeholder on the right or left.

* Make the profile picture easy to replace later.

* A professional headline such as:

    Building Digital Solutions with Technology, Business Insight and AI

* Supporting text:

    I am Sinelizwi Hokwana, a Business Information Technology graduate specialising in Software Development. I build practical web and mobile applications, analyse business problems, and use technology to create meaningful digital solutions.

* Primary CTA button: View My Projects

* Secondary CTA button: View My Certificates

* Include social links for:

    * GitHub

    * LinkedIn

    * Email

Use subtle animations, smooth scrolling, and a polished modern layout.

I am a continuous learner who enjoys turning ideas into functional solutions. My goal is to grow in the technology industry while contributing to projects that make processes more accessible, efficient and innovative.
